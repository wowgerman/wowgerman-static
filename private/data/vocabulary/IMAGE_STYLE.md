# Vocabulary Image Style

## Format

- 4192 × 4192 px PNG, solid white background.
- One subject, isolated (no background scene); add a prop only when the meaning needs it.
- Subject fills the canvas: full width, vertically centred (if taller than wide: full height, horizontally centred).
- Vector, or raster ≥ ~3000 px.

## Style

- Modern flat vector illustration.
- Solid, soft colours with subtle shading; no dark outlines.
- Natural, simple proportions (not chibi/kawaii); simple friendly faces.
- Neutral to positive mood, unless the word itself is negative.
- Reference: Canva "Woman read in cozy armchair. Hobby icon. Resting person".

## Avoid

- Outlined/cartoon kids, chibi, kawaii.
- Watercolour, painterly, textured or hand-drawn (e.g. irasutoya).
- Line art, sketches, silhouettes.
- 3D renders and photos.

## Content

- Nouns: der → man, die → woman, das → kid.
- Show the example sentence's situation, not just the word.
- Male/female variants are separate images (`male-doctor`, `female-doctor`).
- Abstract words: one clear icon or scene that conveys the meaning.
- Series stay consistent: keep the existing style of a set (numbers, months, weekdays, times of day, flags, maps, language speech bubbles).
- Related words (e.g. *schlafen* / *wecken*) may share the same character series when possible; optional, not required.
